      Motion mask tier-1 ignore
      Motion inside these zones is ignored. Mute known false motion — a swaying branch, a street, a neighbour’s window — so it never wakes the detector. Add as many as you need.
        Exclude per-zone mode
        For spots that only ever produce junk (a feeder roof’s underside, a hedge). Click a zone’s mode to switch it.
        Ignore: anything boxed here is dropped as soon as it’s found, before it’s classified — it never becomes a visit and can’t keep the detector busy. For spots no animal can be.
        Hide unrecognized: a visit here that SpeciesNet couldn’t identify is hidden when it ends (an identified bird stays) — kept, and shown again with one click in the viewer.
        Min size (optional): only boxes whose larger side is at least this fraction of the frame count, so a fixed object can be excluded while a small bird passing the same spot isn’t (a feeder roof boxes ~0.05–0.08; a small bird in flight can be ~0.01). Empty: any size.
        Applies to new visits.
